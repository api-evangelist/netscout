AI-driven network operations must adopt vendor-neutral monitoring to achieve true resilience in next-generation networks. Proprietary AI systems locked into single equipment providers create visibility gaps that prevent effective end-to-end service optimization. The author argues that effective autonomous AI agents must be grounded in 3GPP industry standards to avoid triggering unintended consequences, and positions vendor-agnostic observability as essential for communication service providers seeking to reduce costs while maintaining operational flexibility.
