The traditional "more is better" data philosophy led enterprises to over-engineer analytics infrastructure, but NETSCOUT Smart Data marks a shift toward recognizing that raw volume does not guarantee meaningful insights. The concept stems from a critical gap where data may not accurately reflect operational reality, making data quality and relevance more important than sheer quantity for extracting actionable intelligence.
